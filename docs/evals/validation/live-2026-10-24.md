**The run limits.** 90-minute limit and 16,000-token output cap.
- No run timed out, and none was cut at the cap.

**The context.**
- The records give `context_window.tokens` = 262,144, the most gemma4:26b supports, and the run policy's minimum is held against that.
- Ollama actually loaded num_ctx **32,768** in all 19 samples of `ollama ps` ([ollama-gemma4-26b-dev/ollama-ps.log](ollama-gemma4-26b-dev/ollama-ps.log), every 30 s).
