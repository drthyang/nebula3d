- **Unit cell lines.** The switch is named *Unit cell* on every page. The
  dashed lines take a colour the view's colormap lacks (green on inferno,
  magenta on viridis, near-black on RdBu_r's white centre) instead of one
  gray, and their gaps are shorter (4 px dash, 2 px gap), so they read on any
  part of the image.
