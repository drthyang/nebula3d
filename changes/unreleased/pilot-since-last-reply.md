- **NEBULA Pilot: the next reply knows what you changed.** After you apply,
  edit, dismiss or undo a change card, the next reply's context gets one line
  saying what changed since the last reply, with the values before and after
  (for example "you applied punchMinSig 5 → 6; kept ringPooledSectors 72,
  declined 32"). It is sent with that one reply only, so the Pilot does not
  propose a change you turned down as new, and works from the settings now in
  force. It costs about 10 to 30 tokens a decision, and nothing on a reply that
  follows no decision.
