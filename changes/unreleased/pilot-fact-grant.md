- **NEBULA Pilot: Apply on a sample fact's card.** A card for a sample fact
  (the punch's supercell, the modulation), the edge trim, the Laue class and
  the symmetry average's switches, the |Q| band or the magnetic ion now
  applies on your own click, which counts as you stating the value. Edit
  opens the exact values (the modulation's k, order, form and width; the
  band's two edges) and Apply sets the edited ones, checked as the Configure
  page checks them. The click is good for that card's values once: it is
  kept nowhere, so nothing the Pilot writes or sends can apply a fact, and
  its own calls are refused as before. Each such change shows as
  "you (click)" in the Changes list and the report, with Undo.
- **NEBULA Pilot: rings checked from the card.** A ring change held for want
  of a 3D verdict now offers "Check rings in 3D", which judges the rings on
  the outputs as they are: a ring left lets the change apply. "Apply anyway"
  sets it on your click without one.
